Collaborators

Organisations working alongside the ASAP-Bio university partnership to widen access to knowledge, training and professional networks.

Beyond the five university partners, ASAP-Bio collaborates with learning platforms and national professional societies that help extend its reach and enrich its Knowledge Hub.

🌍 Global · Open learning platform

FAO e-learning Academy

A global public good from the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations: free of charge, always accessible and open to everyone, anywhere. The Academy offers 1000+ multilingual, certified courses to around 2 million learners worldwide, spanning agriculture, food security, animal production, natural-resource management and sustainability, all aligned to the Sustainable Development Goals. ASAP-Bio draws on the Academy to complement its own materials with certified courses and to award verifiable digital badges on completion.

Visit elearning.fao.org →
🇪🇹 Ethiopia · Professional society

Ethiopian Society of Animal Production (ESAP)

Ethiopia’s national learned society for animal production, connecting researchers, educators and practitioners across the country. ESAP helps disseminate ASAP-Bio scholarship calls and knowledge resources through its professional network and national conferences.

🇺🇬 Uganda · Professional society

Animal Production Society of Uganda (APSU)

Uganda’s professional society for animal production, linking academia, government and industry. APSU extends the reach of ASAP-Bio in Uganda through its membership network and its national conference, featured in the project News.
