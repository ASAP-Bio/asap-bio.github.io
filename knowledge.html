🧬Genetics foundationsHow inheritance and genetic variation work, the starting point of the breeding track.3 courses · 7 lectures▾
🎓Quantitative Genetics lecture seriesSeven detailed lectures with derivations and worked exercises, by Grum Gebreyesus. Not self-paced courses: these are the lecture chapters themselves.7 lectures▾
1Quantitative traits and distributionsWhy quantitative traits need their own theory: discrete versus metric traits, the many-locus model, the Central Limit Theorem and the infinitesimal model.→ 2Probability and expectationsOutcomes and events, conditional probability, distributions, expectations, variance, covariance, correlation and regression, with the full calculus of means and variances.→ 3The one-locus modelHardy-Weinberg proportions, genotypic values, the average effect of an allele, breeding value and dominance deviation, and the additive and dominance variances.→ 4Relationship and inbreedingIdentity by descent, coancestry, covariance among relatives, Mendelian sampling, Wright's path method, inbreeding depression, the A matrix and assortative mating.→ 5Random genetic driftThe Fisher-Wright model, a full derivation of the drift coefficient, effective population size, Wright's F-statistics, heterosis, and balancing gain against diversity.→ 6SelectionFrom fitness at one locus to the breeder's equation, Fisher's Fundamental Theorem, truncation selection, correlated response, selection experiments and hitchhiking.→ 7Multivariate analysisThe multivariate normal distribution, conditional prediction of genetic values, the two-trait model, decomposing the phenotypic correlation, and genotype-environment interaction.→
