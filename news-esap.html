Featured event · Ethiopia · 29–30 October 2026

Advancing Animal Science Education for Ethiopia’s Livestock Future

The Annual Conference of the Ethiopian Society of Animal Production (ESAP) will be held on 29–30 October 2026, under the theme “Advancing Animal Science Education for Ethiopia’s Livestock Future: Bridging Modern Science and Ethiopian Production Systems for Sustainable Development”. The conference is jointly organised by ESAP and ASAP-Bio (Advancing Sustainable Animal Production and Biodiversity).

Call for papers, sub-themes

Abstracts are invited across the following eight sub-themes:

1

Curriculum Innovation and Modernizing animal science education

2

Genetics, Genomics, and animal breeding

3

Precision Livestock Farming, and Emerging Technologies

4

Nutrition and feeds and Rangeland management

5

Sustainable Livestock Production and Climate Resilience

6

Digital Agriculture and Smart Livestock Systems

7

One Health, Animal Health, and Food Safety

8

Livestock Value Chains, Agribusiness, and Policy

Abstracts and participation in this important event are warmly welcome.
