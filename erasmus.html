Erasmus+ KA171 International Credit Mobility

ASAP-Bio offers international student and staff exchanges through Erasmus+ KA171 funding. This three-year programme (2026–2029) creates learning opportunities for students and staff at Aarhus University and our partner institutions in Ethiopia, Kenya, and Uganda.

Programme overview

Duration

Three years: 1 August 2026 to 31 July 2029.

Total mobilities

62 student and staff exchanges across three partner countries.

Partner institutions

University of Ghana, Bahir Dar University (Ethiopia), Haramaya University (Ethiopia).

Mobility types

Student exchanges for study/thesis work, incoming staff teaching and training, outgoing staff teaching and training.

Who can participate

This programme is open to students and staff at Aarhus University and our three partner institutions. Participants must be:

  • Enrolled students (for study/thesis mobilities) or staff members (for teaching/training mobilities)
  • Nationals of or legally resident in one of the partner countries or Denmark
  • Able to participate in a mobility period of 5–6 months (students) or 1–2 weeks (staff)

Mobility opportunities

Student exchanges

Up to 36 student mobilities for thesis work, research activities, or study periods at partner institutions. Students typically spend 5–6 months abroad, gaining hands-on experience in integrated sustainable animal production systems (ISAPS).

Incoming staff teaching and training

Up to 18 incoming staff mobilities for lecturing, curriculum development, and capacity building at Aarhus University. Typical visits last 5–10 days.

Outgoing staff teaching and training

Up to 8 outgoing staff mobilities for Aarhus University staff to teach, train, and conduct research at partner institutions. Typical visits last 5–10 days.

Mobility allocation by partner

University of Ghana

12 student exchanges (153 days each) + 6 incoming staff + 2 outgoing staff = 20 mobilities

Bahir Dar University (Ethiopia)

12 student exchanges (165 days each) + 6 incoming staff + 3 outgoing staff = 21 mobilities

Haramaya University (Ethiopia)

12 student exchanges (165 days each) + 6 incoming staff + 3 outgoing staff = 21 mobilities

How to apply

Application processes vary depending on your status and role. Contact your institution's Erasmus+ coordinator or the ASAP-Bio coordination team for:

  • Student mobility opportunities and application deadlines
  • Staff mobility opportunities and nomination procedures
  • Learning agreements and administrative requirements
  • Funding details and support available during your mobility

Thematic focus

All mobilities are aligned with the five ISAPS themes, reflecting ASAP-Bio's mission to strengthen teaching and research in integrated sustainable animal production systems:

  • Animal genetics, genomics, and breeding
  • Animal phenomics and precision livestock farming
  • Animal nutrition and feed systems
  • One Health and animal-human-environment interactions
  • Sustainable production and biodiversity

Support during your mobility

The Erasmus+ grant covers travel costs and a daily allowance. Beyond funding, participants receive:

  • Academic and administrative support from both sending and receiving institutions
  • Learning agreement development to ensure academic recognition
  • Pre-departure and post-return guidance
  • Connection to other ASAP-Bio students and staff for collaborative learning
