Master's in Animal Science at Aarhus University

ASAP-Bio invites applications for six fully funded Master's scholarships in Animal Science at Aarhus University, Denmark, starting in 2027. Scholarships are open to candidates from Ethiopia, Kenya, and Uganda who wish to pursue advanced training in integrated, sustainable animal production systems.

The scholarship at a glance

Programme

MSc in Animal Science, taught entirely in English, launching at Aarhus University in 2027.

Specialisations

Choose two or three from: Animal behaviour and welfare, Animal nutrition, Animal care and management, Animal breeding and genetics.

Duration

Two years (full MSc programme, including empirical thesis 40–60 ECTS).

Scholarships available

Six fully funded scholarships for the 2027 intake.

Who can apply

Candidates from Ethiopia, Kenya, and Uganda with a relevant Bachelor's degree in Animal Science, Veterinary Science, or Agriculture with a substantial animal production component.

Gender balance

ASAP-Bio is committed to equal representation. Qualified women are strongly encouraged to apply.

What the scholarship covers

The ASAP-Bio scholarship is designed to cover the costs of studying at Aarhus University, including tuition and support towards travel, living costs, and insurance for the study period. Exact terms will be confirmed to shortlisted candidates in the scholarship agreement.

How to apply: a two-step process

Step 1: ASAP-Bio internal screening (by 30 September 2026)

Submit your documents directly to ASAP-Bio for internal assessment. This is an internal screening to identify the strongest candidates and support them through formal admissions.

Send to: grum.gebreyesus@qgg.au.dk

Subject line: ASAP-Bio MSc Scholarship 2027 - [your full name]

Include:

  • Bachelor's transcript (all courses, credits, grades)
  • Bachelor's degree certificate or proof of expected completion
  • Motivation statement (about one page)
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Copy of passport or national ID
  • English language test result (optional at Step 1, but an advantage)

Shortlisted candidates notified: 15 October 2026

Step 2: Official Aarhus University application (January 2027 deadline)

Shortlisted candidates submit a formal application to Aarhus University through the official admissions system. The application window opens in December 2026.

Check Aarhus University's website for the confirmed application window and deadline.

Note: being shortlisted at Step 1 does not guarantee an admission offer. Final admission decisions rest with AU Admissions, which applies the same standards to all applicants.

Eligibility requirements

Bachelor's degree

You must have completed a Bachelor's degree in a qualifying field by the January 2027 AU application deadline. Qualifying degrees include:

  • Animal Science or Animal Production
  • Veterinary Science, or Agriculture with a substantial animal production component

All degrees must be from an accredited university and contain substantial coursework in subject areas related to animal science.

Previous Master's degrees: Candidates who have previously been enrolled in and completed a Master's degree in any field are not eligible to apply. By submitting an application, candidates declare that they have not completed master's level education in any field. The ASAP-Bio screening team will cross-check publicly available information on this for shortlisted candidates, and those who fail this verification will be disqualified.

Subject coverage

Your Bachelor's programme should contain substantial coursework (equivalent to at least 60 ECTS) across several of these areas:

  • Animal physiology or livestock physiology
  • Microbiology or veterinary microbiology
  • Cell biology or genetics
  • Animal behaviour or ethology
  • Chemistry (organic and/or inorganic)
  • Statistics or biostatistics

You do not need to convert your credits to ECTS. AU Admissions will assess subject-area equivalence individually.

English language proficiency

The MSc Animal Science is taught entirely in English.

  • Step 1 (ASAP-Bio): A formal language test is not required, though it is an advantage to include.
  • Step 2 (Aarhus University): A recognised language test result (e.g. IELTS) is mandatory.

Language test support: If shortlisted after Step 1, ASAP-Bio will either reimburse your test fees (if already passed) or provide financial support for one test attempt.

How applications are assessed

At Step 1, ASAP-Bio assesses applications on:

  • Academic strength
  • Relevance of your Bachelor's degree to integrated sustainable animal production systems (ISAPS)
  • Motivation and career goals
  • Gender balance (commitment to minimum 50% female across cohorts)

Strengthen your application by listing courses in: Biochemistry, Microbiology or veterinary microbiology, Statistics or biostatistics, Cell biology or genetics, Philosophy of science or research methods.
